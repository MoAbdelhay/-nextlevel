  5. N.t.A. 2026/144general
    All clients — annual reminder The studio is once again obliged to remind the public that marketing sells the showing and the showing sells the house. It does not authorise the price, hasten the appraisal, nor hold back the neighbours' opinions. Correspondence on this subject will be filed with the others.

Twice a decade the market files its report and twice a decade it withdraws to reconsider. What it leaves behind is not emptiness but evidence: streets scribbled with sold signs, the comparables combed one way, a bright line of closings where the argument ended.

A listing keeps the only honest clock in this business. It does not divide the week into hours anyone chose; it divides it into shown and not, into the open house crowded and the open house gone quiet. You can set your forecast by it only if your forecast is patient.

The studio measures all of this to the click, and the measuring changes everything, and that is the point of it.
